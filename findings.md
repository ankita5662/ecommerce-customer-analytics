# Key Findings (Olist e-commerce data, delivered orders only)

Revenue figures use total payment value, which includes shipping fees. Currency: Brazilian reais.

## Revenue and growth
- Total revenue was 15.42M reais from 96,477 delivered orders.
- Monthly orders grew from 750 (Jan 2017) to a peak of 7,289 (Nov 2017), then stayed flat at roughly 6,100-7,100 a month through Aug 2018.
- Average order value stayed between about 146 and 176 reais, so growth came from more orders, not larger orders.
- Late 2016 has almost no delivered orders, so trends start from January 2017.

## Where customers are
- Sao Paulo (SP) accounts for about 42% of delivered orders; the top 5 states account for about 77%.

## Delivery and reviews
- Delivery takes about 12.5 days on average. 6,534 delivered orders (about 6.8%) arrived after the estimated date.
- Late orders average 2.27 stars, versus 4.29 for on-time orders (about 2 stars lower). This shows the two go together; it does not prove lateness is the only cause.
- Highest late-delivery rates (states with 500+ orders): MA 17.4%, CE 13.8%, BA 12.2%, RJ 12.1%. Outside SP, RJ has the most late orders in total (1,495).
- Delivered orders average 4.16 stars; other order statuses average 2.0 or below.

## Products
- Top categories by item revenue: health_beauty (1.23M), watches_gifts (1.17M), bed_bath_table (1.02M).

## Customers
- 2,801 of 93,358 customers (about 3.0%) placed more than one delivered order. Almost all revenue comes from first-time buyers.

## Ideas to test next
- Whether the growth plateau in 2018 reflects slower new-customer acquisition.
- Whether late deliveries cluster among sellers far from the customer.
- Whether encouraging a second purchase could lift revenue.
